End-to-End Workflow

Designed for Faculty and Students

Experience an effortless classroom interaction tailored for maximum speed, biometric privacy, and flawless accuracy.

Phase 01

Course Setup & Instant QR

Teachers log in and instantly create subjects with unique section codes. SnapClass automatically generates scannable QR codes and shareable links for rapid student onboarding.

  • Zero-configuration subject setup
  • Automated join codes & QR enrollment
Phase 02

Single-Snap AI Face Attendance

Capture the entire lecture hall via webcam or camera snapshot. Our high-density vision pipeline detects all student faces, compares them against stored embeddings, and marks present attendees in seconds.

  • Multi-face recognition in dense environments
  • Confidence score calculation for every face
Phase 03

Sequential Voice Biometrics Mode

For smaller seminars or audio-verified roll calls, students say "Present" sequentially. The Resemblyzer acoustic model checks voiceprints and flags matches in real-time.

  • Real-time voiceprint embedding matching
  • Voice pitch & timbre verification
Phase 04

Actionable Records & Export

Browse attendance logs, filter by session date and timestamp, review confidence ratings, and export reports to CSV for school administration.

  • Filter by course, date, and attendance status
  • One-click CSV downloads for compliance
Phase 01

Seamless QR Enrollment

Students scan their professor's course QR code or paste a course code. No tedious account creation or email verification loops required.

  • Automatic enrollment directly to roster
  • Cross-platform compatibility on all devices
Phase 02

One-Time Biometric Registration

Register your FaceID and record a 3-second voice sample once. Our neural network extracts high-dimensional biometric vectors and stores them securely in encrypted cloud storage.

  • Direct webcam enrollment in under 10 seconds
  • Biometric vector hashing for total privacy
Phase 03

Personal Attendance Dashboard

Track attendance percentages across all your enrolled subjects in real-time. Review past class check-ins, timestamps, and maintain compliance with ease.

  • Live attendance statistics per course
  • Instant notification upon attendance log
